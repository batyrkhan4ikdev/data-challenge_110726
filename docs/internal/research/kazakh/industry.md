inference. Cross-checks against our own repo: `docs/internal/research/dsml-models.md` (arena/leaderboards),
`docs/internal/research/asr-models.md` (RU/KZ telephony ASR), `docs/internal/research/llm-candidates.md`,

«доступна нам» = already callable from our stack: `alemai/astanahub/alemllm` (облачный API, AlemLLM),

**Read of the table *(inf)*:** the only Kazakh models with public weights **and** a public score are
ISSAI's (KazLLM, Qwen3.5-Kazakh, Qolda) and the community/catalog tier. The two biggest commercial
Kazakh bets — Kaspi's Kasper and Freedom's FreedomSpeech/Freedom Assistant — publish **no weights and
no reproducible numbers**, only executive claims. AlemLLM sits between: open-ish access, self-reported
scores, gated card.

- **Freedom is the most "self-supplied" prospect on the market — the pitch must be about the judge, not the stack.** Freedom already owns transcription (FreedomSpeech, integrated in our repo), a cloned-voice TTS line, a voice assistant with 140 kk/ru commands, an internal broker AI analyst, and is building a $2 bn GPU hub with a public GPU cloud in 2027 ([IR PR](https://ir.freedomholdingcorp.com/press-releases/detail/122/freedom-holding-corp-advances-ai-infrastructure), [forbes 2026-03-03](https://forbes.kz/articles/pogovori-so-mnoyu-turlov-4e724b)). Anything we sell as "ASR + transcription" is already free in-house; the defensible product is **rubric QA over RU/KK/EN code-mixed calls with explainable scores**.
- **The strongest reference in our own segment is ForteBank, and it sells the same number we would.** "From ~1 % of calls reviewed manually to ~100 %" plus >2 M AI-Nur requests, 60 % deflection and a 3,200 person-day/month effect ([digitalbusiness 2026-07-02](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-07-02/ainezamenyaet-lyudey-nomenyaet-protsessi-kak-forte-stroit-bank-novogo-tipa/)) is the benchmark a KZ buyer will cite; Freedom Insurance's own story (65 k calls/month, −25 % operator load) is the closest analogue ([kursiv 2025-09-11](https://kz.kursiv.media/2025-09-11/fvfv-ii-pozvolil-freedom-finance-insurance-izbezhat-mnogokratnogo-uvelicheniya-shtata-koll-centra/)). Position against those outcomes, not against a model brand.
- **Local competitors already speak kk and are explainability-first.** Tanux ("Объясняем каждый балл", score→quote), Aqyl/SOWITA (100 % calls, on-prem, missed demand), Miraitech (per-language standards, disputes re-checked against audio) and Kazteleport (mixed kk-ru speech as its headline advantage) mean a plain "we use an LLM" pitch is already table stakes ([tanuxai.kz](https://tanuxai.kz/), [sowita.kz/aqyl](https://sowita.kz/aqyl), [miraitech](https://aisales.miraitech.kz/kazakh-call-analysis), [kazteleport](https://kazteleport.kz/services/other/rechevaya-analitika)). Our differentiator must be **measured** Kazakh/Russian judge quality (the 24-dialog gold set), because none of these publish any accuracy figure on kk phone audio.
- **Sovereignty and closed-perimeter requirements are now procurement policy, not ideology.** KazLLM was handed to state bodies and runs on domestic compute "for information-security and personal-data compliance" ([standard.kz](https://standard.kz/ru/post/2026_10_ii-na-kazaxskom-iazyke-proekt-kazllm-udostoen-nagrady-amcham-awards-2026-433)); eGov GPT, the QazTech moratorium and the Digital Qazaqstan KPIs push everything on-prem/in-contour ([primeminister 2026-09-11](https://primeminister.kz/ru/news/reviews/realizaciya-poslaniya-prezidenta-2025-kak-kazahstan-perehodit-ot-cifrovyh-proektov-k-edinoy-tehnologicheskoy-ekosisteme-31872)). Halyk and Forte both stress local LLMs / own GPU. Our self-hosted `qwen3.5-35b-a3b-kazakh` on our H200 fits that narrative exactly; a pure GPT/Claude-API answer does not.
- **Kazakh quality is a marketing war, not a measured one — that is the wedge.** "Лучшая в мире модель на казахском" (Kaspi), "лучшее в мире распознавание казахской речи: 8 %" (Freedom), AlemLLM's vendor-only leaderboard, DSML's arena liking Gemma while MC boards like Kazakh-tuned Qwen ([dsml-models.md](../dsml-models.md)) — all self-reported. If we publish a **reproducible** kk/ru judge score on real Freedom calls, we are the first actor in this market to bring third-party-verifiable evidence, and every 2026 board result in KZ (incl. the EMNLP Latin-Kazakh collapse) argues our judge must be validated on our own gold set, not on anyone's model card.

   acquisition/expansion away from being our direct competitor, not only our client.
3. **KazLLM (ISSAI × QazCode/Beeline × Astana Hub, 150 B tokens, 8B/70B)** — the only Kazakh model that
   moved from research into a state asset (handed to government bodies, still periodically fine-tuned,
   AmCham Awards 2026, core exported to Pakistan's UrduLLM) and into telco products (AI Tutor, Aventa,
   AIDA, Mentra); it is the "sovereign" option every B2G buyer will name in a meeting.

5. **ForteBank's speech-analytics platform (~1 % → ~100 % of calls, AI-Nur >2 M requests, >60 %
   deflection, own GPU + local LLM)** — the single closest existing proof of our product's value in a
   KZ bank, and the reference number to beat; note it is internal, not sold, which is exactly the
